## Source-status semantics

- **P0** — primary source: authority/company report, official release, official decision/report, filed report or equivalent.
- **P1** — institutional/historical primary anchor: an institutional historical account grounded in identified scholarship/archival work.
- **S1** — strong secondary source, explicitly attributed where material.

A reachable source is not automatically a true or neutral source. Corporate sources are treated as authoritative for what the organisation publicly reported or documented; evaluative self-statements are not silently converted into independent findings.

## Evidence holds excluded from the public release

Non-public evidence holds, unresolved research claims and claims that depend on non-public audit material remain outside the public source tree.
