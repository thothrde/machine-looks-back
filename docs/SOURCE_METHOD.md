# Source method

Public source classes used in the app:

- **P0**: hard primary source — official authority/company report, official release, law/court/official document.
- **P1**: institutional or scholarly historical primary anchor.
- **S1**: strong secondary source, visibly attributed.

Source-of-truth preference:

`current primary > older primary > strong secondary > LLM summary`

A corporate primary source can establish that the company made a statement; it does not automatically make the statement neutral or independently verified.

Every real case contains:
1. starting point;
2. new evidence;
3. ex-post outcome or current open status;
4. analytical reconstruction;
5. counter-reading;
6. explicit limits of inference and additional evidence needs;
7. source list.
